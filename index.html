My cute lil pattotie 🩷, From the moment I said yes to you on January 1st, 2021, my life stopped being just mine. It became ours. Not perfect, not easy but real, deep, and unforgettable. Even now, it only wants one thing to build more memories with you, no matter how much it hurts or heals. I truly cannot imagine a future without you in it. The thought of a life where you’re not beside me scares me more than any fight we’ve ever had. Those moments undo me. They remind me why I fell, why I stay, and why I will always choose you. No matter how hard life becomes, no matter who stands against us my heart knows only one truth. I choose you. I need you. I love you. And I always will. Your's Lovely, Kuttyma 🩷
